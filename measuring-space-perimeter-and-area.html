Formula Sheet

Keep this section for quick revision before attempting the solutions.

Square

Perimeter = 4 × side
Area = side × side = side²

Rectangle

Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth)
Area = length × breadth

Triangle

Perimeter = side 1 + side 2 + side 3
Area = ½ × base × height

Parallelogram

Area = base × perpendicular height
Perimeter = 2 × (length of one side + length of adjacent side)

Circle

Circumference = 2 × π × radius
Area = π × radius × radius = πr²
Diameter = 2 × radius

Arc and Sector

Arc length = (θ ÷ 360°) × 2πr
Sector area = (θ ÷ 360°) × πr²
Sector perimeter = arc length + 2r

Trapezium

Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height

Heron's Formula

Semi-perimeter: s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2
Area = √[s × (s − a) × (s − b) × (s − c)]

Cyclic Quadrilateral

Semi-perimeter: s = (a + b + c + d) ÷ 2
Brahmagupta's formula:
Area = √[(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)(s − d)]

Units

Perimeter: cm, m, km
Area: cm², m², km²
1 m = 100 cm
1 m² = 10,000 cm²

Important Revision Notes

  • Perimeter uses ordinary length units; area uses square units.
  • For a sector, calculate the arc length first if you need its perimeter.
  • For a segment, subtract the triangle's area from the sector's area.
  • Heron's formula is useful when all three sides of a triangle are known.
  • Always check that all measurements use compatible units.
