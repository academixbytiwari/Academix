Formula Sheet

1. General Sequence

Explicit rule: tₙ = expression involving n
Here, tₙ is the nth term and n is its position.

2. Arithmetic Progression (AP)

Common difference: d = t₂ − t₁
nth term: tₙ = a + (n − 1)d
Recursive rule: t₁ = a; tₙ = tₙ₋₁ + d
Sum of n AP terms: Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n − 1)d]
Also: Sₙ = n/2 × (first term + last term)

3. Sum of First n Natural Numbers

Sₙ = n(n + 1)/2

4. Triangular Numbers

Tₙ = n(n + 1)/2

5. Geometric Progression (GP)

Common ratio: r = t₂/t₁
nth term: tₙ = arⁿ⁻¹
Recursive rule: t₁ = a; tₙ = r × tₙ₋₁

6. Geometric Sum

For r ≠ 1:
Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1)

7. Exponential Growth or Decay

Quantity after n stages = Initial quantity × (growth factor)ⁿ

Use this when the initial quantity is the stage 0 value and the quantity is multiplied by the same factor at every stage.

Symbols: a = first term, d = common difference, r = common ratio, n = term position, tₙ = nth term and Sₙ = sum of the first n terms.

Quick Revision

What is a sequence?
A sequence is an ordered list of numbers in which each number is called a term.
What makes a sequence an AP?
The difference between consecutive terms stays constant.
What makes a sequence a GP?
The ratio of a term to the preceding term stays constant, provided the ratio is defined.
What is the difference between explicit and recursive rules?
An explicit rule calculates a term directly from its position. A recursive rule calculates a term using one or more previous terms.
