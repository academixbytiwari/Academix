Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

At Academix by Tiwari ("Academix", "we", "our" or "us"), accessible at https://academixbytiwari.github.io/Academix/, we respect your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit the website and how it may be used.

1. Information we collect

Academix does not ask visitors to create an account or submit personal information to browse its educational content. However, some information may be collected automatically by website technologies and third-party services, such as:

  • Basic usage information, such as pages viewed, approximate location, browser type, device information and referral source.
  • Technical information needed to operate, protect and understand the performance of the website.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through public or third-party services linked from this website.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use Academix and to improve the website. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information, including interactions with pages and general device or browser information. This information is used in aggregated or statistical form to help us understand website performance.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that websites may store in a browser. Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and usage. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functions or measurement features.

4. Advertising

Academix may consider displaying advertisements in the future. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, those services and their partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable settings. This policy will be updated if our advertising practices change.

For information about how Google uses data on partner sites, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Third-party websites and services

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We do not control their content, security or privacy practices. When you visit an external website, its own privacy policy and terms apply. We encourage you to review them.

6. How we use information

Information collected through website technologies may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Understand traffic and improve educational content and navigation.
  • Identify technical issues, protect the website and help prevent misuse.

7. Data retention and security

Information collected by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and settings. We take reasonable steps within our control to keep the website operating securely, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

Academix provides educational content that may be useful to school students. The website is not intended to require children to provide personal information. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

9. Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and review the privacy controls offered by third-party services, including Google. You may also choose not to use external links or services provided by third parties.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have a privacy-related question, please use the public information or support route provided on the platform where you accessed Academix. We do not collect contact details through this page.
