Academix by Tiwari is an educational website designed to make learning resources easier to find through a simple class, subject and chapter-based structure.

What is Academix?

Academix by Tiwari is a learning-focused platform for students. Its homepage helps learners select their class, subject and chapter so they can navigate to the relevant study material.

Our purpose

Our aim is to keep learning organised and accessible. We want students to spend less time searching through confusing menus and more time understanding the topics they are studying.

What you can find here

  • Class-wise learning navigation.
  • Subject-wise organisation.
  • Chapter-wise access to available study material.

Our approach

We aim to keep the website simple, useful and easy to navigate. Website content and features may be updated over time to improve the learning experience.
